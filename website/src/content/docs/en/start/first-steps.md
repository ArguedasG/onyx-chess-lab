title: First steps
description: Get Onyx ready in a few minutes with engines, a games database, puzzles and, if you like, your Lichess or Chess.com account.
---

The first time you open Onyx, the home screen shows the **First steps** guide. Every step can also be done later from its own section, and you can hide the guide once you no longer need it.

## 1. Install the engines

Press **Install engines**. **Recommended engines** opens with two options:

- **Stockfish:** analysis, evaluations and the strength of the bots. About 80 MB, about a minute.
- **Maia 3:** bots, tournaments and twins that play like people. About 660 MB, several minutes.

**Install both** sets everything up in one step. Maia 3 is installed inside Onyx's data folder: you do not need Python on your system. The requirements for each system are in the [engines guide](../../guides/engines/).

## 2. Add a games database

A games database feeds the opening explorer on the board, preparation against rivals and player cards. Press **Choose a database** and either:

- download a ready-made one from the **Web** tab, or
- convert your own PGN files from the **Local** tab.

The [databases guide](../../guides/databases/) explains both options.

## 3. Download puzzles

Recommended for tactics training: millions of rated Lichess puzzles and sets built from your filters. Your own PGN sets work without this step.

## 4. Connect an account (optional)

Connect Lichess or Chess.com to import your games and use the Lichess explorer. Onyx works without any account.

## Next

- Open the **Board** to analyze a game, or paste a PGN or FEN with **Import PGN or FEN** ([guide](../../guides/analysis/)).
- Go to **Training** to choose between tactics, openings and endgames ([guide](../../guides/training/)).
- In **Players**, gather a rival's games and prepare for them ([guide](../../guides/players/)).

:::tip[Your data]
Onyx keeps your databases, engines and training in the **Documents/Onyx Chess Lab** folder and in the app's data folder. While Onyx is in beta, keep backups of your important PGN files.
:::
