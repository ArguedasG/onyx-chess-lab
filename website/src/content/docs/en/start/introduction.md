Onyx Chess Lab is chess software for Windows, macOS and Linux. It is free, open source (GPLv3) and runs on your computer: you do not need an account to use it.

## What you can do

**The essentials of any serious chess program:**

- Create and download **databases** of millions of games and search them by player, event or position.
- Install **Stockfish** from inside the app or add any **UCI** engine.
- [**Analyze**](../../guides/analysis/) games with the engine, the opening explorer, your databases and your notes.
- Generate **opening reports** and organize your work in [**studies**](../../guides/studies/) with chapters.

**Training connected to what you analyze:**

- **Tactics:** themed sets, timed sessions, a blindfold board and exercises built from your mistakes.
- **Openings:** your repertoire, reviewed with spaced repetition.
- **Endgames:** theoretical positions to play out against an engine or a bot.

**Advanced tools:**

- [**Players**](../../guides/players/): prepare for a rival from their games, or study your own play.
- [**Bots that play like people**](../../guides/bots-and-tournaments/), with Maia 3, and whole tournaments against them.
- [**Books**](../../guides/books/): your chess PDFs beside the board, with their diagrams read into positions.
- [**Model game generator**](../../guides/model-games/): engine games from any position, to see how it is played.

## Where to start

1. [Download Onyx](../../download/) and install it with the guide for your system: [Windows](../../install/windows/), [macOS](../../install/macos/) or [Linux](../../install/linux/).
2. Follow the [first steps](../first-steps/): install the engines, add a database and download puzzles.
3. Start with the guides on [databases](../../guides/databases/), [engines](../../guides/engines/) and [analysis](../../guides/analysis/). Under **Guides** in the sidebar there is one for each section of the app.

## Project status

Onyx is in **public beta**. It is actively tested on Windows x64, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux x64. If you find a problem, [report it on GitHub](https://github.com/ArguedasG/onyx-chess-lab/issues) with your system and Onyx version.

Onyx started as a fork of [En Croissant](https://github.com/franciscoBSalgueiro/en-croissant) and keeps its attribution under the GPLv3. More in [Credits and licences](../../project/credits/).
