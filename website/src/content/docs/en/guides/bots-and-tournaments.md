Onyx's bots play like people of a given level: they make the mistakes a real player of their rating would make, think before moving and each one has its own personality. How they were built and calibrated is explained in [Human-like bots](../../technical/human-bots/).

## Before you start

The bots need two engines: **Maia 3**, which imitates human players, and **Stockfish**, which checks their moves. **Recommended engines** installs both in one click (see [Engines](../engines/)).

## Playing a bot

In **Play**, choose a bot as your opponent. The game panel has, in order:

1. **Opponent:** the chosen bot's card and the **Change** button, which opens the bot explorer.
2. **Time control:** blitz, rapid, classical, no clock or custom.
3. **Game type:** friendly or rated.
4. **Color.**
5. **Help:** no help, warnings, or warnings and hints.

The **explorer** has more than 70 bots, from 800 to 2800. You can search them by name or opening, by rating range and by personality: Striker, Savage, Guardian, Lurker or Balancer. Each card shows the bot's rating at the chosen time control and your score against it.

## Rating and rated games

You have a rating against the bots at each time control: blitz, rapid and classical. It starts as provisional and adjusts with every rated game. Bullet games, games without a clock and games with help are always friendly, and taking back a move turns the game into a friendly one.

Every game is saved in an Onyx database and can be seen from your profile in **Players**, to analyze it like any other.

## Guided games

With **Warnings**, Onyx tells you when you are at a **critical moment**, that is, when one move is clearly better than the rest. With **Warnings and hints** you can first ask which piece to move and then for the move itself.

## Tournaments

In **Tournaments** you play a full tournament against bots. There are four formats: round robin, Swiss, single elimination and double elimination.

- **Templates** fill the tournament around your rating: everyone at your level, you are the favorite, everyone is stronger than you, or a mixed field.
- Games you do not play in are resolved automatically and marked as simulated.
- There is an optional schedule, armageddon in knockouts, withdrawal and PGN export.
- At the end you see your performance and your points against expectation.

You can add the **twin bot** of a player on your list to a tournament (see [Players and preparation](../players/)).
