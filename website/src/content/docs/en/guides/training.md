---
title: Training
description: Tactics, openings and endgames in Onyx, fed by your own games, studies and positions.
---

**Training** has three areas: **Tactics**, **Openings** and **Endgames**. What sets them apart is that they are fed by what you already do in Onyx: a position from the board, a study, a book or your mistakes in a game can become training material.

## Tactics

Puzzle sets by theme, with timed sessions and your own mistakes.

- **Lichess puzzles:** with the puzzle database downloaded (see [first steps](../../start/first-steps/)), you get millions of rated puzzles and can build sets with filters.
- **Your PGN sets:** work without the puzzle database.
- **Modes:** Storm, Streak and Countdown work on any set.
- **Blindfold board:** to train visualisation.

## Openings

Build your repertoire and review it with **spaced repetition**: Onyx works out when you should see each position again so you do not forget it, and shows you the ones due each day.

When you add a position from the board, Onyx tells whether you reached it by the **exact route** of your repertoire or by a **transposition**, so you review the right line.

## Endgames

Theoretical positions to play out against an engine or a bot until the technique is yours. You can add your own positions too.

## From the board

The current-position actions create a reviewed copy of that position for tactics or endgames, or add it to a repertoire. Studies can also send their chapters to training, and **Players** profiles mark critical positions you can train.
