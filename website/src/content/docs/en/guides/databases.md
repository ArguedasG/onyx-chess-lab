title: Databases

---

Databases are your game collections. Besides browsing them directly, they feed the opening explorer on the board, opening reports, preparation against rivals and player cards.

## Add a database

In **Databases**, press the **+** button (Add New). **Add Database** opens with two tabs.

### Web: ready-made databases

Shows the catalog of databases ready to use, with their size and number of games. Press **Install** and Onyx downloads it into your databases folder. The [recommended sources](../../resources/free-resources/) explain where each one comes from and its license.

### Local: your PGN files

1. Enter a **name** and, if you like, a description.
2. Choose the **PGN file**.
3. Press **Convert**.

Onyx imports the games, creates the indexes and finalises the database. You can keep using the app meanwhile: the database appears in the list when it is ready.

## Search games

Open a database to see its games, players and tournaments. You can filter by player, event, date, result or rating, and search by **position**:

- **exact:** games that reach that precise position;
- **partial:** games where the pieces you mark appear, wherever the others are.

Search uses Onyx's own index, built for databases of more than ten million games, and results load page by page so the app stays responsive.

## Opening reports

From a position, Onyx can generate a report with statistics, theory, move orders and transpositions. It can be exported to **HTML** or **PGN**.

## Where they are stored

Databases are stored as `.db3` (SQLite) files in your databases folder, inside **Documents/Onyx Chess Lab**. You can change that folder in **Settings**; Onyx offers to move its contents when you do.
