title: Studies
description: Organise your analysis, lessons and notes into studies with PGN chapters, and turn them into training material.
---

A **study** is an ordered collection of **chapters**. Each chapter is a complete PGN game or analysis, with its comments, annotations and variations. Use them to keep the analysis of your games, prepare a lesson or gather the material for an opening.

Everything is stored on your computer. Onyx does not sync studies with any account.

## Creating a study

In **Studies**, create a new study and give it a name and, if you like, a description. Then add chapters in three ways:

- **Empty chapter**, to start analyzing from scratch.
- **Import a PGN:** every game in the file becomes a chapter.
- **From the board:** in the PGN menu of any open game or analysis, add it to a study.

Studies and chapters can be renamed and reordered.

## Working on a chapter

Opening a chapter loads it on the analysis board, with all its tools: engine, database, plans and position actions. Changes are saved to the study. In the chapter's tab, a button takes you back to the study without closing it.

## From study to training

A study can feed your training without being changed. Onyx always creates a trainable **copy**, and the study remains the source:

- **Tactics:** you choose a position in the chapter and the continuation that is the solution. You decide whether only the main line or the variations as well are accepted.
- **Endgames:** you choose the position, the color you train with and the goal: win, draw or defend.
- **Openings:** the selected chapters become a new repertoire or are added to an existing one, with a preview of the changes.

Onyx never assumes a position is a valid tactic or endgame: you choose it and confirm it.

## Exporting and recovering

- Export a chapter or the whole study as PGN.
- Save or restore a full backup of all your studies.
- A deleted study or chapter goes to the **trash** and can be recovered.
- Each chapter keeps its last 20 versions, in case you need to go back.
